# Two-minute demo script

1. Open NeedleProof and point out the sealed corpus digest in the masthead.
2. Ask: “Report the two assets-under-management dates, the conflicting fee-earning AUM figures, and fee-related earnings.”
3. Let the investigation ledger show several searches and passage reads. Emphasize that these are observable tool actions, not hidden reasoning text.
4. Reveal the verified answer. Open the AUM evidence and show `$146.1 billion` and `$142 billion` attached to different dates.
5. Open the fee-earning AUM conflict and show `$82 billion` beside `$8.2 billion`; NeedleProof preserves both instead of guessing.
6. Explain that the separate headcount golden test requires four distinct successful searches and returns bounded “not found” wording rather than claiming nonexistence.
7. Download the canonical JSON receipt and point to result IDs/scores, quotations, request IDs, timing, hashes, and corpus digest.
8. Run the receipt challenge with a modified quote and show deterministic rejection.
9. Close with: “Inspect the evidence, download the receipt, or ask the corpus another question.”
